## Summary

I'm an engineering manager with eleven years in software, the last four
leading teams. I currently manage two product engineering teams (12
engineers) at a healthcare scheduling company. Before management I was a
full-stack and backend engineer working mostly in Ruby on Rails and
React. I care about building teams that ship steadily without burning
out, and I measure myself by how many of my engineers grow into the
next role.

the Scheduling and Patient Messaging teams. I hire, run one-on-ones and
performance reviews, and partner with product and design on quarterly
planning. In the last year we rebuilt appointment reminders as an

18%, and moved the teams from irregular big releases to daily
deployments. I grew my organisation from 6 to 12 engineers, and two
engineers I managed were promoted to senior. I set up our incident

**Senior Software Engineer, then Tech Lead, Clinicflow, Austin (2019 to
2022).** I designed the clinic availability engine in Ruby on Rails and
PostgreSQL, led a team of four as tech lead from 2021, and worked with
compliance on our HIPAA controls.

**Software Engineer, Brightpath Learning, Dallas (2015 to 2019).** I
built features across a Rails and React education platform used by
200 school districts, and took over ownership of the payments
integration.
