# Priya Raman

Engineering Lead, .NET Platforms · London, United Kingdom
priya.raman@example.com

## Profile

I have spent twelve years building and running .NET systems for
insurance and banking, starting as a graduate developer writing C#
services and growing into leading a platform group. For the past three
years my role has been mostly people and delivery leadership: I manage
two teams totalling eleven engineers, set technical direction for our
policy administration platform, and work with product and risk partners
on what we build next. I still review architecture proposals and the
occasional pull request, but I no longer write production code day to
day. I am looking for a lead role where I can keep shaping architecture
while growing engineers.

## Career

**Engineering Lead** · Harbourline Insurance · London · *2023 – Present*

I lead the Policy Platform group of two teams. In that time we moved the
quoting engine from a monolithic ASP.NET application on Windows servers
to containerised .NET 8 services on Azure Kubernetes Service, without a
customer-facing outage. I introduced quarterly architecture reviews,
an engineering career framework that the wider department later adopted,
and an on-call model that cut out-of-hours pages by around 40%. I hire,
run performance reviews and coach three senior engineers who now lead
their own workstreams.

**Senior Software Engineer, later Technical Lead** · Harbourline
Insurance · London · *2018 – 2023*

I designed the event-driven integration between the policy system and
the claims system using Azure Service Bus, owned the SQL Server data
model for policies, and led a squad of five developers as technical lead
from 2021. I wrote much of the C# domain model for pricing rules and set
up the team's automated testing with xUnit and SpecFlow.

**Software Developer** · Calder & Finch Bank · Leeds · *2014 – 2018*

I joined on the graduate programme and built C# and WCF services for
internal payment operations, later migrating several of them to
ASP.NET Web API. I maintained SQL Server stored procedures and reports
used by the operations team.
